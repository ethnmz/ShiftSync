# Demo script

The submitted video is at most 2 minutes. The rehearsal below is optional and is not the submission cut. Speak the lines; do not read the API.

Fictional site only. No real phone numbers on screen.

## Submitted cut (≤ 2:00)

| Time | What happens | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title card | "Shift A thinks they explained it. Shift B thinks they understood. Those are not the same." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Outgoing voice note: "The UPS vendor is coming back tomorrow. Everything should be fine." | "That sentence is the whole handoff a lot of crews get." |
| 0:35–1:05 | One interview question: what is still unfinished. Answer: the vendor still has to verify. The fact stays unfinished. The hedged "should be fine" is shown as reported, not confirmed. | "We ask one missing question. We do not turn a hedge into done." |
| 1:05–1:25 | Record shows who verifies as UNASSIGNED. Signoff is blocked. A person types a name and a time. | "If nobody owns the unknown, the app will not sign the handoff." |
| 1:25–1:50 | Incoming answer: "Maintenance is done." Correction quotes Shift A: the vendor still has to verify. | "He heard done. The note said not done. The check catches that." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Signed record with the quote. The footer from `docs/UI.md` is visible. | "We keep the words. We do not certify the building." |
